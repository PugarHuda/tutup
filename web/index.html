🔒 Tutup sealed bids when the market is closed

The market is closed.
The auction isn't.

Stock Tokens trade 24/7, but their price feeds stop at the close and nobody can mint or burn on weekends. Tutup runs sealed-bid batch auctions for those hours: bid in secret, reveal together, everyone pays one price. Being first wins nothing.
