List a lot

Listing burns 100 $WIF. Your lot is escrowed until settlement; whatever doesn't sell comes back to you.

How it works

01 · commit

Seal your quantity and price as a hash and post a 50 $WIF bond. Nobody sees your bid, not even the seller.

02 · reveal

Open your bid and escrow payment, and your bond comes back. If you never reveal, the bond is burned.

03 · settle

The highest bids fill first and every winner pays the lowest winning price. Losers get everything back.
