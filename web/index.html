WHY TUTUP

Stock Tokens trade 24/7, but the market behind them doesn't. Tutup gives those hours a fair price.

The feeds stop. The official Chainlink feed for each Stock Token updates 24/5 (regular, extended and overnight sessions), then stays frozen from Friday night to Sunday night, on market holidays, and whenever a corporate action sets oraclePaused().

Minting stops. Authorised Participants can only mint and burn from Monday 02:00 to Saturday 02:00 CET. Outside that window nobody can arbitrage a token back to its share.

The race starts. Robinhood Chain orders transactions first come, first served. When quotes go thin, whoever is fastest takes the price.

Tutup ends the race. Every bid is sealed until bidding closes, so arrival order doesn't matter, and every winner pays the same clearing price.

HOW IT WORKS

Three phases. Nothing is visible until bidding closes, and nothing pays you for being first.

01 · COMMIT
Seal it.

You post a hash of your quantity and price, plus a 50 $WIF bond. The seller and other bidders see only that you bid.

✓keccak256(id, you, qty, price, salt)
✓your salt never leaves your browser
02 · REVEAL
Open it.

Once bidding closes, you open your bid and escrow payment, and your bond comes back. A bid that is never revealed loses its bond to the burn.

✓fake bids cost real $WIF
✓payment escrowed, so a bid can't be backed out of
03 · SETTLE
One price.

The highest bids fill first and everyone who wins pays the lowest winning bid. Losers are refunded in full, and anything unsold goes back to the seller.

✓ties break on a hash, not on who arrived first
✓Chainlink price recorded next to the clearing price
